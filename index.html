01 / Evidence Measured blind · real public corpora

The evidence, with its test context

  • ~97% retrieval hit-rate across a 480-question blind evaluation over a 700-document, 29,506-chunk public corpus. Raw artifacts hash-manifested.
  • 76.9% strict factual accuracy in the shipped configuration on the same sealed evaluation; 91% on single-fact questions and 92% on entity disambiguation.
  • 16 / 16 model fabrication attempts held back by the provenance gate in that run; none reached a user as Verified.
  • 156 real, public NASA documents in the live demonstration corpus — every citation opens a document you can download and check yourself.

Internally executed blind protocol on real, public corpora: the engine never saw the sealed key; a separate source-only oracle authored it and a different model family judged the answers. Independent external replication is a pilot objective. Figures are from interim development hardware.

02 / The gate Deterministic · versioned acceptance policy

The guarantee is enforced in code, not hoped for

Every output is provenance-gated. The gate applies a deterministic, versioned containment policy before a claim can be shown as Verified; the cited source remains open for inspection. Cortessa never certifies — the engineer signs off.

Verified

Shown only when a claim is provably a near-verbatim span of a cited source.

Draft

Unprovable claims are shown as Draft — never passed off as fact.

Refused

When the fact is not present, the system refuses rather than guess.

04 / Effectivity S1000D · configuration-aware

The right revision for the right variant

Supersession is a claim about time — Rev E replaced Rev D. Effectivity is a claim about configuration — Rev D still applies to hulls 1–4, Rev E from hull 5, both current at once. Serving the wrong one to the wrong variant is a safety event, and a search engine cannot tell the difference.

  • Configuration-aware answers. For S1000D technical publications, an answer is bound to the product configuration it applies to — modules for a different variant are withheld and reported, not silently blended in.
  • Undecidable is flagged, never guessed. Where applicability cannot be decided from the data, the answer says so — the same refusal ethos as the provenance gate.
  • Benchmarked. Unfiltered retrieval answered 4 of 8 questions and leaked the wrong variant four times. With the effectivity filter active: 8 of 8 answered and 0 wrong-variant leaks, on a disclosed, purpose-authored S1000D corpus.
05 / Capabilities One guarantee across all three

Three capabilities, one guarantee

A/ Audit Shipped

Lead capability

Cross-references your whole corpus and surfaces where documents disagree — numeric conflicts, supersession, broken references, stale content — as a cited findings list for an engineer to adjudicate.

Recall 1.0 and precision 1.0 on the seeded regression set, across four seeded error classes. Separately, a blind run over a real NASA programme document family returned 15 / 15 genuine findings.

B/ Ask Shipped

Cited answers

Natural-language questions answered from your corpus, every sentence cited. It refuses when the fact is not present.

~97% retrieval and 76.9% strict factual accuracy across the 480-question blind evaluation; all 16 model fabrication attempts in that run were held back. In a separate exam, 12 / 12 out-of-corpus questions were refused with 0 fabrications.

C/ Author In development

Sign-off-ready briefings

Turns the corpus into a cited, sign-off-ready briefing, where the engineer accepts, revises, or rejects each drafted claim. In development — additional testing underway.

06 / Deployment Disconnected · single executable

Defence is the proving ground

Sovereignty, provenance, air-gap operation: a product that clears that bar clears it for everyone else. Cortessa runs disconnected, on a dedicated sovereign workstation on the analyst's desk — a single executable; no server, no cloud, no internet dependency. Nothing leaves the machine to answer a question.

The same guarantee travels to civil aerospace, nuclear, energy, rail, pharma, legal and government estates. One product, one guarantee, many document estates.

Explore sectors & use cases

  • On-device (standard) the model and the whole ingestion pipeline are resident on the box and run full time; nothing leaves the machine.
  • Retrieval only the floor beneath it — ranked source passages with their citations, no model in the loop. Always available; never a failure.
  • In-region cloud — off by default a future option for a site whose policy later permits one. It ships switched off, and can be locked out entirely so it cannot be selected at all. Opt-in and logged if it is ever enabled (UK, London entry region).
