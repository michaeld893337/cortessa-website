Sectors

One product, one guarantee, many document estates

Cortessa is defence-first by design. The guarantee that clears a controlled defence environment — sovereign, air-gapped, provenance-gated — clears the bar for every other regulated estate. The promise never changes; only the pain and the paperwork do.

Defence is the proving ground

Defence programmes run on controlled document estates that are too large to cross-check by hand. Quoting a superseded value — a coupling rated to 30 bar where the current standard says 45 — costs rework, generates findings and carries safety-case risk. Cloud assistants are barred at the boundary: data would leave, and they confidently invent specifications. Enterprise search returns passages with no provenance guarantee. Manual review is accurate but scales only with headcount.

Cortessa runs disconnected as a single executable, audits the estate for conflicts and supersession, and gates every claim to a cited source span — or refuses. That is why defence is the launchpad: sovereignty, provenance and air-gap operation are non-negotiable there, and a product that meets them meets them anywhere.

On customers. Cortessa is built to the constraints of a controlled defence environment. We do not name customers or imply a relationship we cannot evidence.

Expansion estates

The same guarantee travels to any estate where the current, correct, cited fact matters and cloud AI is barred at the boundary. Each estate keeps the promise and swaps in its own pain.

Government

Controlled records and policy estates that cannot leave the boundary but still need to be cross-checked and current.

How to read the evidence. The performance figures cited across this site were measured blind on real, public corpora (NASA/NTRS and arXiv document sets); raw run artifacts are hash-manifested. They are not sector-specific benchmarks. A proof point for any particular estate is established with the customer during a pilot before it is claimed.

The guarantee that carries across all of them

  • Provenance you can trace. Verified / Draft / Refused — a value not in the source cannot pass as Verified.
  • Sovereign and offline. Nothing leaves the machine to answer; air-gap-safe, no external calls.
  • Audit the whole estate. Cross-document conflict detection hands engineers cited, adjudicable findings.
  • No infrastructure beyond the desk. One dedicated workstation, one executable — no server room, no cloud.
