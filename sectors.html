Sectors

One product, one guarantee, many document estates

Cortessa is defence-first by design. The guarantee that clears a controlled defence environment — sovereign, air-gapped, provenance-gated — clears the bar for every other regulated estate. The promise never changes; only the pain and the paperwork do.

01 / Defence The proving ground

Defence is the proving ground

Defence programmes run on controlled document estates that are too large to cross-check by hand. Quoting a superseded value — a coupling rated to 30 bar where the current standard says 45 — costs rework, generates findings and carries safety-case risk. Cloud assistants are barred at the boundary: data would leave, and they confidently invent specifications. Enterprise search returns passages with no provenance guarantee. Manual review is accurate but scales only with headcount.

Each Cortessa engineer box runs locally, audits its controlled corpus for conflicts and supersession, and gates every claim to a cited source span — or refuses. The target architecture is designed to carry approved revisions through departmental nodes and an on-site hub. Defence makes sovereignty, provenance, air-gap operation and human engineering authority non-negotiable; those constraints shape the product for every other regulated estate.

On customers Cortessa is built to the constraints of a controlled defence environment. We do not name customers or imply a relationship we cannot evidence.

02 / Estates Where the guarantee travels

Expansion estates

The same guarantee travels to any estate where the current, correct, cited fact matters and cloud AI is barred at the boundary. Each estate keeps the promise and swaps in its own pain — none is an afterthought, and the guarantee does not dilute as it travels.

Global banking

Group policy, local addenda, legal-entity obligations and assurance evidence must reconcile before attestation.

Architecture fit: local policy and risk teams work on self-sufficient boxes; departmental nodes preserve entity and function scope; authorised revisions move without implying a cross-border public network.

Civil aerospace

Airworthiness and type-certification evidence spread across revisions that must stay internally consistent and current.

Architecture fit: design, continuing-airworthiness and configuration teams retain local working context while approved revisions follow the site's engineering authority route.

Nuclear

A configuration-controlled safety case that must stay current and defensible across a vast document estate, permanently, under audit.

Architecture fit: design engineering, configuration control and safety assurance remain distinct authorities joined by signed, scoped revisions—not automatic plant or safety decisions.

Energy

Asset-integrity and compliance documentation where a superseded figure is a finding waiting to happen.

Architecture fit: maintenance, integrity and engineering-assurance teams can preserve departmental scope while sharing approved documentary changes inside the site boundary.

Rail

Standards conformance across a large asset base, where references and figures must reconcile across many documents.

Architecture fit: fleet, standards and engineering-acceptance teams work locally, with governed revision lineage across the internal authority chain.

Pharmaceutical

Validated document control under regulatory audit, where provenance and traceability are the whole point.

Architecture fit: quality, validation and change-control teams retain separate authority while approved revisions move through a controlled site route; validated use still requires representative assurance.

Legal

Large matter and disclosure sets where an answer has to be traceable to the exact source, not a plausible summary.

Architecture fit: matter and practice-team boundaries remain explicit. Nothing in the architecture implies cross-client sharing or replaces legal judgement.

Government

Controlled records and policy estates that cannot leave the boundary but still need to be cross-checked and current.

Architecture fit: policy, records and assurance teams can keep local context while approved revisions move through enrolled departmental scope; public authority remains with accountable officials.

Policing

Policy, case and disclosure estates where accountable review depends on the correct source, revision and recorded human judgement.

Architecture fit: operational, professional-standards and disclosure teams retain bounded working context while approved documentary revisions move through authorised on-site routes; policing authority remains with accountable officers and staff.

How to read the evidence The performance figures cited across this site were measured blind on real, public corpora (NASA/NTRS and arXiv document sets); raw run artifacts are hash-manifested. They are not sector-specific benchmarks. A proof point for any particular estate is established with the customer during a pilot before it is claimed.

03 / The pattern Constant across estates

The guarantee that carries across all of them

  • Provenance you can trace. Verified / Draft / Refused — the deterministic gate records whether a claim is anchored to its cited source; it does not certify correctness.
  • Sovereign and offline. Analysis stays on-box; governed revisions remain inside the site boundary.
  • Audit the whole estate. Cross-document conflict detection hands engineers cited, adjudicable findings.
  • One governed site model. Self-sufficient engineer boxes connect through departmental nodes to an on-site hub; production rollout remains in development.
